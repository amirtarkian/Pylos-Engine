How to hold the cube while entering it
  1. F (front): whichever face is toward you. Keep this grip for the whole solve.
  2. U (up): look down at the top face; the row nearest you is the bottom row of U.
  3. R, B, L: turn the whole cube left one quarter at a time; enter each face as it faces you, top still up. Turn it back afterwards.
  4. D (down): tip the cube so you see the bottom; the row touching the front face is the top row of D.
